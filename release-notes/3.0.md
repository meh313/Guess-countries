**What's new in 3.0**

- **A map for every country**: see where it is in the country sheet ("Where it is"), on the back of its flashcard, on Learn cards and after a miss, with its capital marked and a small world inset. Map data from Natural Earth (public domain), bundled in the app: it works offline like everything else.
- **New quiz mode, Map → Country**: a country is highlighted on the map, pick its name. Wrong answers come from the same region, so it gets harder as you learn. Placing a country is its own skill with its own reviews, shown on the country sheet and as a Located tile on Progress; the Due tile counts map reviews too.
- **Ready for the store**: a signed app bundle alongside the APK on every release, a privacy statement (nothing leaves the device) linked from About, and store screenshots and listing text in the repository.
