**What's new in 3.4**

- **Ads in the free version**: one short full-screen ad when you leave the score screen of every third quiz, never during a question, on launch, in Learn or in the flashcards, and none in your first two days. The ads come from Google AdMob, asked for at the general-audience (G) rating with the gambling, alcohol, dating and similar categories blocked; where the law gives you a say, a consent form appears first and *Settings › Ads and purchases › Ad privacy options* brings it back.
- **Remove ads**: a one-time purchase under *Settings › Ads and purchases* ends the ads for good and makes the 50/50 hint free. *Restore purchase* brings it back on a new phone.
- **A 50/50 hint in the quiz**: two wrong answers go, and the question is worth half its points (and counts as light evidence for your reviews). Watch a short ad for it, or have it free with Remove ads. Not in the blitz or the typed-name mode.
- **Privacy**: the app now has the network permission for its ads; your progress still never leaves the device. The privacy statement and the Play Data safety form say what the ad request carries. Phones without Google Play services get no ads and nothing for sale.
