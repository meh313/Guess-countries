**What's new in 3.2**

- **Reviews due by the day**: a review is due for the whole day its interval ends on, so a flag learnt in the evening is due all of the next day. The Today card counts the capitals and the map reviews due as well as the flags, offers each in turn, and counts the flags Learn 5 new showed you.
- **A truer map**: land outside the app's countries, such as Greenland, Puerto Rico and Hong Kong, is drawn as land rather than sea, and Lesotho, San Marino and the Vatican are left out of the country around them. Crimea is drawn in Ukraine, the Golan Heights in Syria and the Åland Islands in Finland, and Morocco stops at Western Sahara; Palestine's capital is marked in East Jerusalem, and France's borders no longer list the Netherlands.
- **Fairer quizzes**: a typed answer that is another country's name is not forgiven as a typo (Zambia is not the Gambia), and "The Gambia", "The Bahamas" and "Naoero" count. Indonesia and Monaco, whose flags the app draws alike, are never offered for each other, and the capital quiz no longer offers as a wrong answer a city the app itself names as one of the country's capitals, such as Cotonou for Benin or Colombo for Sri Lanka. A quiz straight after another asks other countries, and Retry of a Learn quiz asks all its questions, the map question included.
- **The clocks stop** while the app is in the background: the speed round and the blitz no longer time out or finish while you are away.
- **Your streak keeps its days**: each past day is judged by the daily goal you had then, so a new goal no longer wipes the streak or inflates it.
- **Undo the last grade** of a study session from its summary, before you move on.
- **Translations read right**: counts take the right plural in every language, continents are named in your language in the quiz and on the cards, and a language set by an import or in the phone's settings is the one on screen. Reading aloud is in English throughout, like its voice.
- **Smaller fixes**: the widget updates after a reset, an import or a change of goal or language; each day's reminder alerts even when yesterday's is still showing; the miss labels and the Explore title are readable in the dark theme, and the status bar icons follow the app's theme on Android 7 to 11; the speaker comes back in Flashcards and the country sheet after installing voice data; France's population is the UN's 2024 figure and Eritrea's triangle is the flag's own blue.
