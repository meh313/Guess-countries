**What's new in 2.3**

- **A quiz that does its reviews**: countries due for review come first (up to seven of ten), then your weak spots, then at most three new ones per quiz and fifteen a day. Yesterday's countries are no longer held back just because you saw them recently.
- **Misses come back**: up to two countries you got wrong are asked again at the end of the quiz with fresh options. Retry shuffles the answers.
- **Fairer evidence**: a miss under time pressure (Speed Round, Blitz) drops one step instead of resetting you to the start; a miss in a normal quiz drops two. Grading a due flashcard "Good" clears it from the due list. The longest review gaps are now 21 and 60 days, so what you know well comes back less often.
- **Continents**: the continent quiz keeps its own score, and countries on two continents (Cyprus, Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Turkey, Kazakhstan, Russia, Egypt) count as right either way.
- **Importing a backup can no longer break the app**: damaged, foreign, newer or oversized files are refused with a plain reason, and the app checks the file before it asks you to confirm.
- **Reminders fire on time** even when the phone is dozing, and survive a reboot or a time-zone change.
- **Study sessions survive** the app being closed in the background, and Back ends them cleanly.
- **Polish**: tapping a continent on Progress shows the countries you still have to learn there; the quiz works in landscape and on short screens; grade buttons and small labels are readable in the dark theme; the Progress tiles, pips and log read better and the log can be unfolded; the About page shows only the notices a reader needs; wording made consistent throughout.
- **Catalog**: look-alike groups and old names added to search (Siam, Dahomey, Upper Volta, Abyssinia, Gold Coast, Aotearoa, DR Congo, KSA), five fun facts replaced, populations rounded the usual way.
