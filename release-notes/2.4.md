**What's new in 2.4**

- **Learn 5 new**: five flags you have not seen yet, each with a one-line "spot it" cue and the flag it is most often confused with, then a short quiz over the five. Start it from the Today card or the Flashcards study menu.
- **Spot it**: every flag now comes with a cue on what to look for, shown on the flashcard back, in the country sheet and after a miss; for the classic look-alike pairs (Chad and Romania, Monaco and Indonesia, the Nordic crosses, the Central American blues…) a tell-apart tip says what settles them.
- **Today**: a card on Explore and Progress with what is due, what you learnt today and your daily goal (5, 10 or 20 items, in Settings). One button does the right next thing: review what is due, or learn five new.
- **Start here**: on first run the app asks which continent to begin with; quizzes introduce its flags first. Change it any time in Settings.
- **Streaks follow the goal**: a day counts when you meet your goal, or finish a quiz or study session of ten; a four-question quiz no longer keeps a streak alive. Study sessions of five cards now count too.
- **Capitals**: a capital study deck (flag and name on the front, capital on the back), wrong answers from the same region, and the classic traps (Sydney, Istanbul, Lagos, Zurich…) among the options.
- **Progress**: a Learning tile, how many reviews are coming up tomorrow and this week, the pairs you confuse most with their tell-apart tips, and a tap on any quiz in the log opens its full result again.
