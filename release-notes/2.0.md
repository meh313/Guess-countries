**What's new in 2.0**

- **A new way of learning.** Every country is tracked per skill (its flag, its capital) on a spaced-repetition schedule: a right answer when the review is due moves the country up a box (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days), a wrong one sends it back. Quizzes favour the countries you have not seen or that are due, and avoid the ones they just asked about. A new **Due today** scope reviews what is due; **Weak spots** follows the skill of the mode.
- **A score screen that teaches.** Every miss is listed with the flag you picked beside the right one, with **Retry** and **Practise mistakes**. A wrong text answer shows the two flags side by side at once; after a flag pick every flag is named.
- **Study sessions.** Flashcards now browse with Previous and Next, and *Study* runs a session of up to 20 cards (due today, weak spots or this deck) graded Again / Good / Easy after the flip, with Undo. Ten or more cards count as practice for the streak.
- **Progress** shows mastered flags and capitals and what is due today. A quiz now survives the app being killed in the background.

**After the upgrade** your progress is kept, but fewer flags will show as *mastered*: the old single score counted three right answers in one sitting (or three taps on "Mastered!") as mastery. A country at 75 points lands in box 2 and needs two more due reviews on separate days; one at 100 points lands in box 3 and keeps its badge while it is reviewed on time.
