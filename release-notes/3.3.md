**What's new in 3.3**

- **A Home screen**: the app opens on one big Play button that starts a ten-question flag quiz at once, with your level and XP, your streak, the Today card and your badges.
- **A daily challenge**: the same ten questions for everyone on a given day, in a different quiz mode each day, with 50 bonus XP the first time you finish it. It can be played once a day.
- **Game feel**: confetti for a good result (not when the system's animations are off), XP and level-ups on the score screen, and 11 badges to earn. XP, levels and badges are worked out from your quiz log, so they follow an import and a reset.
- **A new app icon**: the origami map pin.
