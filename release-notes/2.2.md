**What's new in 2.2**

- **Tablets and wide screens**: a navigation rail along the side instead of the bottom bar, and the quiz, score, Progress, Settings and About pages keep a comfortable reading width in the middle instead of stretching across.
- **Large fonts**: nothing is cut off any more at the biggest font settings; buttons and the Explore banner grow with the text, the Progress tiles stack, and the tab labels shrink instead of breaking.
- **TalkBack**: section titles are headings, the mode cards and chip rows are groups, text answers are buttons, the quiz timers can be asked for the seconds left, and the blitz waits for Next while touch exploration is on.
- **Right-to-left layouts** mirror properly, and every word the app shows now lives in its string resources, which is the groundwork for translations.
- No more white flash when the app opens: the first frame uses the theme background in light and dark.
- Explore: the sort menu ticks the current order, and the keyboard's action on the search field is Search.
