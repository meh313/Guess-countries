**What's new in 3.1**

- **Four languages**: the screens come in French, Spanish, German and Arabic besides English; pick one under Settings › Language (or in the phone's per-app language setting on Android 13+). Country names, capitals and facts stay in English.
- **Flag → Typed name**: a quiz mode with no options; type the name. Short forms such as UK, USA or DRC count, and one typo is forgiven.
- **A map question in every Learn quiz**: after the ten flag questions, one of the five is to be found on the map.
- **Borders**: every country sheet lists the countries it shares a land border with; a tap opens that one. The map quiz takes its wrong answers from the neighbours first.
- **Sound effects and vibration** for quiz answers, each with its own switch under Settings › Feedback.
- **A home-screen widget**: the reviews due now, your streak and today's items against the goal, with a Practise button.
- Under the hood: the look-alike flags and the sounds are checked in CI, and a smoke test runs on a real Android emulator after every merge.
