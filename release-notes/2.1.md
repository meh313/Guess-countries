**What's new in 2.1**

- **Settings** (the gear on Progress): theme (system, light, dark) and wallpaper colours on Android 12+; the pace of the timed quizzes (normal, relaxed, no limit); **Export** and **Import** of your progress as a file; **Reset progress**.
- **About**: where the facts come from and how they were checked, what the catalog contains, the licences, and the privacy terms (nothing leaves the device). A country's detail sheet lists the pages its facts were checked against.
- **Your progress per country**: two pips on every country card (flag, capital: grey, amber, green) and a "Your progress" row in the detail sheet with the box and the next review. Explore gets Unseen / Learning / Mastered chips, and a tap on a continent on Progress opens Explore on that continent's countries still to learn.
- Android's backup now names exactly what it keeps: the progress database and the settings.
